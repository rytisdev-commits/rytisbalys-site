Questions I hear before we start

Do I need server-side tracking?

If you pay for ads, usually yes. Browser-only tracking drops purchases to ad blockers and iOS, and the smaller the budget, the more each lost signal costs. I say first whether it fixes your gap at all.

What access do you need?

Read access first: Events Manager, GA4, GTM and the store. Publish access only once the fix is agreed, and only for that step.

How fast?

Most single fixes take 1 to 2 days after access, plus a day to verify. Bigger builds get a date after the look inside.

What if the numbers are actually fine?

Then I tell you, and you owe nothing. A gap under about 10% is normal loss, not a tracking problem.

Do you do calls?
